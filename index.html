Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a photo to a province on the map?

Tap any province on the map to open the area editor. Tap "Add Photo" and pick an image from your library. You can crop and rotate the photo to fit the province shape.

How do I merge multiple provinces into one area?

In the map creation screen, long-press a province and drag across adjacent ones to combine them into a single piece. The merged area shares one photo.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Premium → "Restore Purchases". As long as you sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, your Premium will be restored at no extra charge.

How do I back up or transfer my maps?

Open Settings → Backup → "Export Backup" to save a backup file. On the new device, open Settings → Backup → "Import Backup" and select the file.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the Tripiece purchase. If you need our help, email us using the address above.

Why does the app ask for photo library access?

Tripiece needs access to your photo library so you can select travel photos to place on the map. We do not upload, share, or transmit your photos. All data stays on your device.

Why does the app show an App Tracking Transparency prompt?

If you allow tracking, ad networks may show ads relevant to your travel interests. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. Premium users see no ads.
